Privacy Policy

Toehold

Toehold is designed to collect nothing about you.

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no network requests at all. Puzzles are generated on your device. Nothing leaves it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your current puzzle and its progress
  • Your settings, such as whether mistake checking is on

These are stored using the system's own on-device storage, never leave your device, and are deleted when you delete the app. (Declared in the privacy manifest under Apple reason code CA92.1.)

Network access

The app makes no network requests of any kind. There is no server, no account system, and no puzzle download — every puzzle is generated on your device by the app itself.

Third-party services

None. No analytics SDKs, no advertising, no crash reporters, no trackers.

Children

Toehold is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.
