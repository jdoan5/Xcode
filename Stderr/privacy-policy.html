Privacy Policy

Stderr

Stderr is a catalogue of Java and Python programs that look correct and are not. It is built to collect nothing about you — no account, no sign-up, no tracking, no ads, and no analytics.

Summary in one line: The app has no networking code at all. It works identically in Airplane Mode, and nothing you do inside it leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account to create and no email to enter. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no third-party software of any kind — the app uses only Apple's own frameworks and has no Swift Package dependencies.

The app does not use the internet

This is stronger than a promise not to send anything. The compiled app contains no networking code: no URLSession, no WKWebView, no CFNetwork. A script checks the source for those symbols before every release and fails the build if one appears. Put the device in Airplane Mode and every entry still works.

What is stored, and where

Nothing beyond the app itself. There is no progress to save, no history, no bookmarks and no settings that leave your device. The catalogue ships inside the app and is read-only.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear on this page. Since the app collects nothing, there is not much that could change.
