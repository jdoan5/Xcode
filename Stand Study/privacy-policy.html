Stand Study · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Applies to Stand Study 1.0 and later on iPhone and iPad

Stand Study collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no server and no network connection: what you keep in the app stays on your device, and the developer never receives any of it.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your sets. Their names and order, and Stand Study's own copies of the pictures you add to them.
  • Your log. When each session started and ended, its plan, which reference each pose showed, how long you drew it, whether you skipped it, and any drawing photos you chose to save.
  • Your settings. Chime, warning tick and haptics, the view aids new sessions start with, and the choices you last made on the Draw tab.
  • A session in progress. If you leave the app in the middle of a session, it is set aside on the device so it can still reach your log if the system closes the app. It is removed once the session is saved.

Sets and the log live in a database inside Stand Study's private area on the device, with the pictures and drawing photos as image files next to it and the settings among the app's preferences. If you back up your iPhone or iPad, the backup includes this data along with everything else on the device. Stand Study keeps no copy anywhere else.

Photos and the camera

You add pictures with the system photo picker, so Stand Study only ever receives the ones you select and cannot browse your library. Each one is copied into the app and re-encoded as a JPEG from its pixels alone, so no location, camera or date details come with it. The original in Photos is not changed.

The camera is used only when you tap Take Photo (or Retake) in Compare to photograph a drawing, and iOS asks your permission the first time. You can decline and use Compare with a photo from your library instead. A drawing photo is kept only if you tap Save to log, and is stored the same way, without metadata. Stand Study never saves anything to your photo library.

The network

Stand Study does not connect to the internet. It has no server to talk to, downloads nothing and uploads nothing, and every screen behaves identically with Airplane Mode on. The Sample set's artworks are part of the app itself.

No tracking, no third parties

  • No analytics, ads, crash reports or tracking of any kind.
  • No code from other companies: the app is built only on Apple's own frameworks.
  • No access to your location, contacts, microphone or health data.

Deleting your data

Swipe a session in the Log to delete it with its drawing photos. Removing a picture from a set, or deleting a set, deletes Stand Study's copies of those pictures too. Deleting the app deletes everything it keeps.

Children

Stand Study gathers no information from anybody, children included.
