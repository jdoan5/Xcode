What Stand Study does

It shows reference images one at a time on a timer while you draw on paper. You choose the images and a plan, such as ten poses of one minute; it counts down, chimes and moves on. View aids change how the reference looks, Compare lays a photo of your drawing over it, and the Log keeps each session. There is no drawing canvas: your sketchbook is the canvas.

Your first session

  1. Open the Draw tab. The Sample set is ticked under References.
  2. Under Plan, choose Warm-up (ten poses of 30 seconds) or any other plan.
  3. Tap Start, set the phone where you can see it, and draw.
  4. When the plan ends, or you stop early with End, a summary shows what you drew. Tap Save to Log.

Questions

How do I add my own reference images?

In the Library tab, tap New Set and give it a name. Open the set, tap Add Images and pick as many pictures as you like from Photos. Stand Study keeps its own copy of each, without location or camera details, and the originals in Photos are left as they are. Then tick the set on the Draw tab. You can draw from several sets at once, with or without the Sample set.

Why does the Sample set use museum art?

So you can start a session the moment the app is installed, with references whose rights are clear. The 28 images are public-domain artworks from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, which releases them under Creative Commons Zero (CC0) through its Open Access program. They were picked for clear, clothed figures in many poses: dancers, workers, standing and seated figures, in prints, paintings, drawings and sculpture. Every image is credited in Settings › About Stand Study. Stand Study is independent of the museum and is not endorsed by it.

How does Compare work?

During a pose, tap Compare. (When a session ends, the summary also lets you attach a drawing to any pose you drew.) Photograph your drawing, or choose a photo of it, and it appears on top of the reference. Move the opacity slider to fade between the two, drag to move your drawing and pinch to resize it until they line up, then look for where the proportions differ. Side by side shows the two next to each other. A running pose pauses while Compare is open. Save to log keeps the photo with that pose; Cancel keeps nothing.

What do the view aids do?
  • Grayscale removes color so you can judge light and dark.
  • Notan reduces the image to two or three values. The threshold decides where light turns to dark.
  • Grid draws thirds, quarters or diagonals over the image.
  • Mirror flips the reference left to right.

Change them at any time during a session. Settings › Default View Aids chooses how new sessions start.

How does Stand Study use iPhone Duo's fold?

The layout is designed around the fold. Rest the phone partly folded on a desk and the upper half holds the reference, facing you, while the timer and controls sit on the lower half. Hold it partly open with the fold running down the middle and the reference takes one half, the controls the other. Open flat or closed, the screen is arranged by its size, the same way as on other iPhones and iPads. Folding only moves things around: every feature is available however the phone is held.

Does the timer keep going if I leave the app?

Yes. The time left is worked out from the clock, so when you come back the session is on the right pose with the right time. Tap Pause first if you want it to wait for you. No chime or tick plays while Stand Study is in the background.

How does the Untimed plan move on?

Untimed poses have no countdown. Tap Next when you have finished each drawing.

Can I turn off the sounds?

Yes. In Settings, switch off the end-of-pose chime, the 10-second warning tick or haptics, in any combination. The chime and tick follow your phone's silent mode, so turn silent mode off if you want to hear them.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe a session left in the Log to delete it and its drawing photos. In the Library, remove pictures from a set, or delete a whole set, and Stand Study's copies are deleted too. Deleting the app deletes everything it has kept.

Does it need the internet, or collect anything?

No to both. Stand Study works offline, makes no network connections and has no account. The details are in the privacy policy.
