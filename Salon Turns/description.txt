A walk-in comes through the door. Whose client is it?

Salon Turns answers on one screen, and shows its working: "Lisa: fewest turns (1). Tied with Jenny; Lisa has waited longer." The rule is plain enough for everyone at the stations to check, so turns stop depending on memory or on who speaks first.

HOW THE NEXT TECHNICIAN IS CHOSEN
- The next walk-in goes to the free technician with the fewest turns today. If you prefer, choose the lowest service total entered instead.
- A tie goes to whoever has been free the longest, then to whoever checked in first.
- A half service counts as half a turn, so a quick polish change need not count the same as a full pedicure.
- A client who asks for someone by name is a request. Requests are counted separately and, unless you switch it on, do not use up a turn.
- Someone who arrives late can start level with the lowest count on the board, so they are not handed every walk-in that follows. Or they can start from zero.

AT THE DESK
- Check the team in with one tap as they arrive.
- Give the walk-in to the person named, as a full or a half service. A wrong tap can be undone for a few seconds.
- Mark a service done and, if you like, type in the service price and the tip.
- A break takes someone out of the line; coming back keeps their count.

WHEN SOMEONE ELSE SHOULD TAKE IT
Sometimes a client needs a particular skill. Giving a walk-in out of turn asks for a reason and, once you have set one, the owner PIN. Adjusting a count and cancelling an entry work the same way.

THE DAY'S LOG
Every action is listed with its time. An entry made in error is voided with a reason and stays in the log, crossed out, so the record shows both what happened and what was corrected.

CLOSING TIME
The Tally lists each technician's services, full and half turns, requests, and the service and tip amounts staff entered, with totals. Share it as a CSV spreadsheet or a PDF. Earlier days stay available to look back on.

WHAT IT IS NOT
- Not payroll. It does not work out wages or commission, and it does not take or send money. The Tally only adds up numbers staff typed in.
- No customer data. The app keeps the names you give your technicians and the amounts entered. It never asks for a client's name, phone number or email.
- Works offline. No login, no online setup: Salon Turns makes no internet connections at all. Its records live on the iPad.

MADE FOR THE COUNTER
- Large buttons that work in landscape and portrait.
- An option to keep the screen awake while the app is open.
- VoiceOver labels on the board's cards and buttons.
- Try it before entering your own team: Settings > Load sample day fills today with a sample Saturday (and sets the owner PIN to 1234). When you are ready to start for real, Clear today empties today's log; then rename or archive the sample technicians on the Team screen and set your own PIN.
